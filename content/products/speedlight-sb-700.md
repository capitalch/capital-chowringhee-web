The **Nikon Speedlight SB-700** is a versatile, compact hot-shoe flash built for portrait photographers and event shooters who need reliable bounce-and-swivel flexibility without stepping up to a full professional flagship unit.

Optical Advanced Wireless Lighting lets it work as an off-camera remote flash or as a commander for other compatible Speedlights, opening the door to simple multi-light setups for portraits. Three selectable illumination patterns let you tune the beam for even coverage, dramatic center-weighting, or wide-area fill.

A dependable, affordable flash for photographers building out their first off-camera lighting kit.
