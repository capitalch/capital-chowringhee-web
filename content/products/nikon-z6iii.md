The **Nikon Z6III** brings a partially-stacked sensor — a first at this price point — to Nikon's most popular all-rounder line, delivering faster readout, deeper buffers and cleaner rolling-shutter performance than any Z6 before it.

It's built equally for stills and video: 20 fps blackout-free RAW bursts for action and events, and internal 6K/60p RAW video for filmmakers, all backed by the same deep-learning autofocus tracking found in the Z8 and Z9. The electronic viewfinder is the brightest Nikon has shipped, staying clearly visible even under harsh outdoor light.

For wedding photographers, event shooters, and hybrid content creators in Kolkata's studios, the Z6III is the sweet spot of capability, size and price — and it's one of our most demonstrated cameras in-store.
