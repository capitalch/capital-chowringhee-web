The **NIKKOR Z 70-200mm f/2.8 VR S** rounds out the professional f/2.8 trinity, covering portrait-compression through reach for sideline sports and event coverage. In-lens vibration reduction combines with in-body stabilisation on Z-series bodies for confident handheld shooting even in low light.

Twin stepping motors keep autofocus fast and near-silent — critical for video work and for tracking unpredictable subjects — while a removable tripod collar and full weather sealing make it as comfortable on a monopod at a stadium as it is on location for a wedding.

A staple for sports, wildlife-edge and portrait photographers who need f/2.8 reach without compromise.
