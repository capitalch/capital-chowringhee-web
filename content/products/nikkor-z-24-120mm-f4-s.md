The **NIKKOR Z 24-120mm f/4 S** trades a stop of aperture for a much wider focal range than the 24-70mm f/2.8, covering wide-angle to short telephoto in a single, relatively compact lens — making it the lens many photographers leave on their camera for travel, events and everyday shooting.

S-Line optics keep it sharp across the frame at every focal length, and a constant f/4 aperture means exposure stays consistent as you zoom. Combined with in-body stabilisation on Z-series bodies, it's a genuinely capable one-lens-kit for photographers who don't want to be changing lenses mid-event.

We frequently recommend this as the primary lens for Z5II and Z6III buyers who want maximum flexibility from a single lens.
