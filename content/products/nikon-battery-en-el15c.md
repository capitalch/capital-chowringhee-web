The **EN-EL15C** is Nikon's current-generation Li-ion battery, compatible with most full-frame Z-series bodies as well as many recent DSLRs — a sensible spare to keep in your bag for a full day of shooting.

Genuine Nikon batteries also support in-camera USB-C charging and full battery-health reporting, unlike third-party alternatives.
