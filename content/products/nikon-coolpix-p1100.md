The **Nikon Coolpix P1100** is built for reach — its 125x optical zoom lens goes from wide-angle out to an extraordinary 3000mm equivalent, all without changing lenses.

A favourite for moon photography, distant wildlife and airshow spotters who want maximum reach without the cost or bulk of interchangeable super-telephoto lenses.
