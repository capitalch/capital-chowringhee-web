The **Nikon Z8** packs the same stacked sensor, EXPEED 7 processor and blackout-free shooting experience as the flagship Z9 into a smaller, lighter body without an integrated grip — making it the camera of choice for photographers who need Z9-level performance but travel light.

Deep-learning autofocus tracks nine subject types automatically, from people and eyes to birds, cars and planes, and continues to nail focus at up to 20 frames per second in RAW. On the video side, the Z8 shoots internal 8K/30p and up to 4K/120p, with support for N-RAW and ProRes RAW for serious hybrid shooters.

Dual card slots — one CFexpress Type B and one UHS-II SD — give flexibility for backup recording or overflow, while full weather sealing keeps it working through Kolkata's monsoon shoots.
