The **Nikon Z5II** is the most affordable route into Nikon's full-frame Z-series, and it inherits far more from the flagship line than its price suggests — the same EXPEED 7 processor and deep-learning autofocus system found in the Z6III and Z8, in a lighter, budget-friendly body.

It's an ideal first full-frame camera for enthusiasts moving up from APS-C, or for content creators who want full-frame depth and low-light performance without flagship pricing. In-body stabilisation and reliable subject tracking make it a forgiving, confidence-inspiring camera to learn on.

Pair it with the NIKKOR Z 24-70mm f/4 S kit lens for a genuinely capable full-frame travel and everyday kit — available as a bundle in-store.
