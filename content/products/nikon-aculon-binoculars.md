Multilayer coatings across the optical path improve contrast and colour fidelity, while a rugged, rubber-armoured body stands up to outdoor use. Long eye relief makes them comfortable to use with or without eyeglasses.

A popular recommendation for customers heading out on wildlife trips, sports events, or simply wanting a reliable everyday pair of binoculars.
