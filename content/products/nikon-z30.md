The **Nikon Z30** is designed from the ground up for vloggers and content creators rather than traditional stills photography — there's no viewfinder at all, keeping the body small and light while a fully-articulating screen faces forward for easy self-recording framing.

Reliable autofocus, a built-in stereo microphone with wind reduction, and up to two hours of continuous 4K recording on battery make it a genuinely practical everyday vlogging camera rather than a stills camera pressed into video duty.
