The **Nikon Speedlight SB-5000** is Nikon's professional flagship flash, built for photographers who fire hundreds of frames at a wedding or event and cannot afford a flash that overheats or lags. A built-in cooling system allows sustained rapid-fire use well beyond what standard Speedlights can handle.

Radio Advanced Wireless Lighting extends reliable off-camera flash control out to 30 metres, even without line of sight — a major advantage over optical-only systems when working around obstacles or in bright daylight.

The natural choice for professional wedding and event photographers building a serious multi-flash lighting setup.
