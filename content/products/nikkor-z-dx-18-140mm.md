The **NIKKOR Z DX 18-140mm f/3.5-6.3 VR** is the superzoom companion for Z50II and Z30 owners who want a single lens covering wide-angle to short telephoto without ever swapping glass — from group shots and interiors right through to portraits and distant details.

In-lens vibration reduction keeps handheld shots steady across the zoom range, and the compact, lightweight build keeps DX bodies feeling genuinely portable for travel and everyday carry.

An excellent step-up lens for Z50II or Z30 owners who've outgrown their kit lens and want more reach without adding bulk.
