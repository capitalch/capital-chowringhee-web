The **Nikon Zf** wraps genuinely modern full-frame mirrorless performance in the styling of Nikon's classic FM2 film camera — milled dials for shutter speed, ISO and exposure compensation sit alongside a fully modern 24.5MP sensor, EXPEED 7 processing and deep-learning autofocus.

It's a camera built for photographers who want to shoot deliberately: manual dials up front, in-body stabilisation rated to 8 stops for handheld low-light work, and a range of compact Z-mount prime lenses that match its retro proportions.
