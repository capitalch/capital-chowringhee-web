The **NIKKOR Z 24-70mm f/2.8 S** is the professional standard zoom of the Z-mount system — the lens most working photographers reach for first. It covers wide-angle environmental shots through short-telephoto portraits at a constant f/2.8, with S-Line optical quality that stays sharp into the corners even wide open.

Dual stepping motors deliver fast, quiet, precise autofocus for both stills and video, and full weather sealing with a fluorine-coated front element keeps it working through demanding outdoor shoots.

For wedding, event and editorial photographers, this is typically the second purchase after a Z-series body — and one of the most frequently bundled lenses we sell in-store.
