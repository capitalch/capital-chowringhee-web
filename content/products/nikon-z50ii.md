The **Nikon Z50II** brings the EXPEED 7 processor and deep-learning autofocus from Nikon's professional cameras down into a compact, affordable APS-C body — meaning subject detection and tracking that used to be flagship-only is now available to first-time mirrorless buyers.

A dedicated Picture Control button lets creators cycle through in-camera looks on the fly, and native vertical-video support makes it a strong choice for reels and social content, in addition to solid 4K oversampled footage for anyone stepping up from a smartphone.

Paired with the compact NIKKOR Z DX 16-50mm or 18-140mm lenses, the Z50II makes an excellent lightweight travel and everyday camera — one of our most recommended cameras for students and first-time buyers.
