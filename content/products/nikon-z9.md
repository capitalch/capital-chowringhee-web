The **Nikon Z9** is Nikon's flagship professional mirrorless camera, built for photographers and filmmakers who cannot afford to miss a moment. It was the first Nikon body to ship without a mechanical shutter entirely, relying on an ultra-fast sensor readout that eliminates blackout in the viewfinder and rolling-shutter distortion in stills.

Inside is a 45.7-megapixel stacked CMOS sensor paired with the EXPEED 7 processor, powering deep-learning subject detection that recognises people, animals, birds, vehicles and more — locking focus and tracking through crowded, fast-moving scenes.

For video, the Z9 shoots internal 8K/60p RAW and 4K up to 120p, making it as serious a cinema tool as it is a sports and wildlife stills camera. The integrated vertical grip, extensive weather sealing, and dual CFexpress Type B card slots make it the natural choice for professional assignments where reliability is non-negotiable.
